Terms & Refunds — M1

These are operating terms for the validation build, not final jurisdiction-specific legal terms. Live sales remain disabled until business identity, support contact, and applicable consumer-law details are confirmed.

The offer

The planned M1 offer is a one-time $149 purchase for a 30-day personalized relationship decision-support program. It is not a subscription and does not auto-renew.

What is included

The program may include a relationship situation summary, one concrete next action at a time, practical wording when useful, outcome check-ins, and adjustments based on what the customer reports actually happened.

Not therapy or guaranteed results

Relationship Reset is not psychotherapy, medical care, diagnosis, legal advice, emergency support, or a promise that a relationship will improve or continue. Recommendations cannot control or predict another person's behavior.

Safety limitation

If the situation may involve violence, threats, coercive control, stalking, forced sexual contact, or fear for safety, Relationship Reset should not provide ordinary reconciliation or confrontation advice. The normal paid flow may be stopped.

Refund approach for the first M1 customers

If you request cancellation before the first personalized paid Reset has been delivered, the intended M1 policy is to provide a full refund. After personalized delivery has begun, contact support and the request will be reviewed in good faith based on what was delivered and any technical or service problem. Nothing in this policy is intended to waive consumer rights that apply by law.

Delivery

For the first M1 customers, payment and case reference are manually verified before the first paid action is delivered. The first personalized paid Reset is intended to be delivered within 24 hours after both successful payment verification and receipt of the customer’s diagnostic intake.

Acceptable use

Do not use the service to stalk, threaten, coerce, deceive, manipulate, harass, or repeatedly contact someone who has clearly asked not to be contacted.

Changes before live launch

Business identity, support contact, governing terms, final refund wording, and any required tax/consumer disclosures will be completed before live payments are enabled.
