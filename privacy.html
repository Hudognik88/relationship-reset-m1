Privacy Notice — M1

This page describes the current validation build. Live payments are not enabled yet. A final business contact and jurisdiction-specific notice will be added before live sales.

What the prototype collects

The free diagnostic asks about your relationship situation, what happened, what you did, what seemed to help or make things worse, and your immediate goal. A safety question is used to stop ordinary relationship advice when the situation may involve violence, threats, coercion, stalking, forced sexual contact, or fear for safety.

Where diagnostic answers are kept before purchase

In the current M1 build, raw diagnostic answers are kept in your browser session using session storage. They are not placed in Stripe metadata, payment URLs, UTM tags, or advertising analytics. Closing the browser tab/session may remove them.

What Stripe receives

Stripe receives payment information, a random opaque case reference (case_id), and non-sensitive campaign attribution such as UTM or creator code when present. The case reference contains no relationship meaning.

What we do not use relationship text for

For M1, free-text relationship answers are not used for advertising targeting, retargeting, creator attribution, or sale of personal data.

After purchase

For the first M1 customers, payment is manually verified before fulfillment begins. If you choose to submit your diagnostic after purchase, it will be used only to deliver the requested 30-day service and evaluate product usefulness. The minimum necessary information should be retained.

Deletion and retention

The live policy will state a concrete retention period only after the technical deletion process is implemented. We will not promise an automatic deletion schedule the product cannot enforce.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. Stripe handles card and payment data under its own privacy and security practices.

Safety

Relationship Reset is not therapy, emergency support, or a crisis service. If a diagnostic indicates possible violence, coercion, or immediate safety risk, the ordinary recommendation and sales flow is stopped.

Contact

A dedicated support/privacy contact will be published here before live payments are enabled. During sandbox testing, communication remains in the tester invitation channel.
